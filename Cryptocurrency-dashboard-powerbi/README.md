# Cryptocurrency Dashboard (Power BI)  
**by Sejal Dewangan**

---

## 📘 Project Overview  
The **Cryptocurrency Dashboard** is an interactive **Power BI** project designed to visualize and analyze real-time and historical data on various cryptocurrencies.  
It provides insights into **market performance**, **price trends**, **trading volumes**, and **volatility** across different time periods — enabling users to make informed, data-driven decisions.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies  
- **Power BI:** Data visualization and dashboard creation  
- **Python / API Integration:** Data extraction from cryptocurrency APIs (optional)  
- **Excel / CSV:** Data preprocessing and transformation  
- **Data Sources:** Public cryptocurrency datasets (CoinMarketCap, CoinGecko, etc.)

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features  

### 1. **Market Overview**  
- Displays current **cryptocurrency prices**, **market capitalization**, and **24-hour volume**.  
- Allows comparison of top-performing coins such as **Bitcoin, Ethereum, Binance Coin, and others**.  

### 2. **Price Trend Analysis**  
- Line and area charts track historical **price fluctuations** over time.  
- Enables filtering by **time period**, **coin type**, or **market segment**.  

### 3. **Volatility & Performance Metrics**  
- Visualizes **price volatility** and **percentage change** over selected intervals.  
- Highlights the most and least volatile assets for risk assessment.  

### 4. **Trading Volume Insights**  
- Shows trading activity across exchanges and timeframes.  
- Identifies coins with **rising trading interest** or **market momentum**.  

### 5. **Portfolio & Watchlist View (if implemented)**  
- Personalized tracking of selected cryptocurrencies.  
- Aggregated portfolio statistics including average gain/loss and distribution.  

---

## 📈 Key Insights  

1. **Market Leaders:** Bitcoin and Ethereum dominate market capitalization, accounting for the majority of the total crypto market.  
2. **Volatility Patterns:** Altcoins show higher price fluctuations compared to established assets.  
3. **Trading Volume Trends:** Sudden volume spikes often precede major price movements.  
4. **Correlation:** Some coins display strong correlation with Bitcoin trends, useful for forecasting strategies.

## 💡 Business Use Cases  

- **Investors:** Track portfolio value, analyze historical trends, and identify high-performing assets.  
- **Analysts:** Monitor market volatility, liquidity, and overall crypto ecosystem health.  
- **Businesses:** Gain insights into crypto adoption trends for payment or investment strategy decisions.
